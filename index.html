About Me

I’m Minsun Cho, an M.A. student in Educational Technology at Seoul National University and a member of the Technology Enhanced Learning Design (TELD) Lab. My undergraduate thesis explored a question-based physics lesson using ChatGPT in a thermodynamics unit.

In my current work, I interview teachers about their beliefs, observe digital learning in schools, and help design XR learning activities. These experiences have led me to a broader question about how students make sense of science over time.

Research Goal

How do students’ scientific ideas change as they ask questions, use evidence, and discuss explanations?

For doctoral study, I want to understand how classroom activities and learning technologies can help students test and revise their ideas while keeping them actively involved in the reasoning.
